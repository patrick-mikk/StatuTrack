StatuTrack

Visual change tracker for Canadian federal law.

StatuTrack treats the official XML consolidation of Canadian Acts and Regulations as a version archive, and renders what changed between any two versions inline — in the style of a tracked-changes document — alongside a clean reader view of the current consolidated text.
